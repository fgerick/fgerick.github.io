## Welcome!

![placeholder](assets/avatar_github.jpg#avatar) I'm a researcher in geophysical fluid dynamics, currently working as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoctoral fellow at the [University of Leeds](https://environment.leeds.ac.uk/institute-geophysics-tectonics/doc/deep-earth). I study hydromagnetic modes in liquid and conducting planetary cores and their influence on the rotation and magnetic field of the planet. Besides my fellowship project [Wave-based imaging of Earth’s liquid core from space (WIMECS)](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101203618) I am also involved in the [4D-Earth-Swarm](https://4d-earth-swarm.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/) collaboration.

My [Codes](/codes/) are publicly available and from time to time I try to write some instructive words about how to use these codes in [Posts](/posts/).
